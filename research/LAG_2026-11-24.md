# LAG_2026-11-24: "When i type in text boxes it is laggy. Parlayapi site and its animations were very laggy. Pressing back was fast. Switching between tabs has to reload sites and this is slow. Scrolling is laggy" (owner, 2026-11-24, PERF24)
Read from the two alpha103 uploads (504 + 289 events) and the 14-day history. Public repo: site kinds only. The owner's settings in those uploads: Software drawing ON, Scroll feel 0 ("Standard (Firefox's own)", the least pre-drawing; the default is 1 "Smoother"), Tabs kept ready Few (2), Calmer redrawing ON in the first upload and OFF in the second, the three alpha103 experiments OFF.

## What the numbers say, per complaint
1. **Tab switching reloads and is slow.** `tabs hibernate tab=74 live=3 max=2 avail=534MB` (and 509, 458, 944 MB, 294 MB): the tab put to sleep each time is tab 74, the heavy chat page, which then costs `paint=13067ms done=22799ms` to bring back (first load of the day: `fcp=19026ms`, `done=36758ms`). The other two live tabs were PRIVATE tabs (tabs 75 to 77, `private=true`), which can never sleep, yet "Tabs kept ready: Few (2)" counted them: the regular tab paid for them, with up to 944 MB free. Back, in contrast, is fast: 6 of 8 Backs were instant (kept by the engine).
2. **The clip page is very laggy.** `video quality ...: frames=327 dropped=132 (40 %)`, `frames=223 dropped=220 (99 %)` on the 480x910 clip, three or four clips playing within seconds of each other; the app's own window stalled in its draw call (`ui slow 826ms: HardwareRenderer.nSyncAndDrawFrame`, `ui late-beat 982ms`). alpha102's limiter allowed TWO clips at once; the counters show it acted (`videoclips.deco.cap=4`), but two clips are already too many for this tablet in Firefox with software drawing.
3. **Typing is laggy.** `win typing-page`: the page's own next frame after a key took `resp=98ms` on average and up to `respworst=1183ms` (6 of 40 keys over 150 ms; 9 of 24 in another window), while the keyboard's own hand-over was fast (`avg=16 to 24 ms`). Windows on light pages were fine (`resp=24 to 52 ms`). So the wait is the page's frames, not our keyboard.
4. **Scrolling is laggy.** Same root as 3: page frames after a load come at about 6 a second on the pages measured (`PAGE ANIMATION: 78.9 % late, median 6.4 frames a second, longest gap 600 ms`, 14 pages). Scroll feel 0 is the setting with the least pre-drawing; the app's own UI had `jankFrames=302` in the busy upload.

## What alpha104 changes (all JVM / Chromium tested; NOT run on the tablet)
- **Tabs:** the Settings number limits REGULAR tabs only; private tabs no longer count against it; the memory rule (kernel's available memory) limits all live tabs and now also caps a fixed "Many"; when a tab has to sleep, the cheapest to bring back goes first and a tab whose last load took 8 s or more (the chat page) is the last resort (core LiveBudget.sleepPlan, LiveBudgetTest, OwnerRulesTest perf24).
- **Clips:** ONE decorative clip at a time (was two), and none while the page is scrolled; they come back 0.45 s after the scrolling stops (decoclips.js; web-tests/decoclips.test.js, 8 tests in real Chromium).
- **Typing and scrolling:** no separate code change: both follow the page's frame delay. The clip change removes the biggest measured source of late frames on that page; the rest is the page's own JavaScript on four weak cores. Setting to try (no code): Settings > Appearance > Scroll feel > Smoother (default) instead of Standard.

## Not fixed, said plainly
- A heavy chat page still takes 13 to 23 s to paint when it is brought back after it slept (software drawing; page scripts). The new rule makes it sleep far less often; it does not make the reload faster. The 4-hour start screen does not apply here (that is for a cold start).
- Typing in the heavy chat page stays limited by the page.
