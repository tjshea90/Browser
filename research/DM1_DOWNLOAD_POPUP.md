# DM1: the download manager pops up by itself (owner message 2026-11-18)

Owner, verbatim: "Also the download manager pops up on browser restart or other times, the download manager shouldn't pop up by itself unless there is a new download".

Two things on screen can be called "the download manager": the full Downloads list, and the small download window (with the "Download started" banner) at the top right. Both had a way to appear without a new download. Found by reading the code (nothing was run on the tablet; the emulator cannot tap a real notification from a dead process):

## 1. The full Downloads list: a replayed notification intent (BrowserActivity)
`Notifier.openApp` builds the launch intent of every download notification (the ongoing "Downloads in progress" one and the "Finished" ones) with the extra `OPEN_DOWNLOADS`. `BrowserActivity.onCreate` opened the list whenever its intent had that extra, and `onNewIntent` kept it with `setIntent(intent)`. Android keeps the launch intent on the task and starts the activity AGAIN with it after a crash, after the process was taken for memory, or from Recents. So after ONE tap on a notification, every later restart of the app could open Downloads by itself ("on browser restart or other times"). This also fits the first message of this session: the app was ended without a trace (CR1), and Android restarted it with the old intent.
Fix: the flag is read through `core/download/ManagerIntent.shouldOpen(extra, restoredState, fromHistory)`: only an intent that is not a re-creation (`savedInstanceState == null`) and not launched from history opens the list; the extra is also removed from the intent once read. Tests: `ManagerIntentTest` (4), `OwnerRulesTest.dm1TheDownloadsPanelOnlyComesUpForANewDownloadOrARealTap`.

## 2. The small window: old rows looked new (DownloadPopup + MiniTracker)
- `DownloadPopup.attach()` called `tracker.begin(DownloadController.views())` in `onStart`, but the controller reads the rows of the last run on its own thread (`e.restore(); st.restore(); e.resumeInterrupted(); ...`). On a cold start the list was still empty at `begin`, then every stored row (finished, paused, or carried on by itself) appeared as a "new id": a "Download started" banner and a window full of old rows. That is "pops up on browser restart".
- A download that was already there and then started moving (the engine carries interrupted downloads on by itself after a restart, a stalled one resumes when the network is back) was added to the window by design ("it joins the window when it starts moving"). That is "or other times".
Fix: `DownloadController.restored` (true after the restore and the carry-on) and one `MiniTracker` for the whole process (`DownloadController.mini`, with `miniHidden`); the window starts the tracker only once `restored`, everything in the list at that moment is old news and is never listed; an old row joins the window only when the PERSON asked for it to move (`DownloadController.resume / restart / resumeAll` call `mini.requested(id)`). A genuinely new download (a tap, "Save video", the phone's) is still announced and listed. Tests: `MiniTrackerTest` (rewritten rules + 2 new), `OwnerRulesTest`.

## Not proven on the tablet
Both fixes are rules plus unit tests; the real notification replay after a crash and the start-up race need the tablet. After the update: tap a download notification once, close the app from Recents, open it again from Recents (and once after it was ended by Android): the Downloads list must stay closed; restart the browser with a paused download in the list: no banner, no window.
