# The download engines on a hotspot that drops (RX3-D1, D2, D3) - notes and measurements
Newest section last. ASCII only. Review text: research/REVIEW-EXTERNAL-3.md section 7.

## 1. RX3-D1 - an outage waits, it does not fail (alpha43)
Problem (read in the code 2026-11-14): `StreamEngine.fetchLoop` gave a part six tries (waits 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 s) and then threw `Failed(Reason.NETWORK)`, which stopped the whole video download; the single-file `Engine.waitOutOutage` refused to wait when the download had more than one connection (`job.alive > 1`), so a dip that hit all of them also ended in FAILED. The app deliberately does not use `NET_CAPABILITY_VALIDATED` (a working hotspot lacks it), so the network gate stays "online" exactly in the blip the owner's phone hotspot makes (the phone loses data, the tablet still sees the hotspot, the resolver answers at once).
Change:
- `StreamEngine.exhausted(job)`: a part that used up its tries at the transport level (no HTTP answer): a download that never got a byte fails fast as before (a typo in the address, a dead host: `Failed(NETWORK)`); one that did and whose bytes stopped for `Config.outageQuietMs` (8 s) throws `Outage`; while other parts still bring bytes the part simply gets another round.
- The worker turns `Outage` into `stopState = WAITING_NETWORK` for the whole job (the other workers stop with it, the staged parts and the output stay); `settle` sets the ladder (`Config.outageBaseMs` 10 s doubling to `outageCapMs` 60 s, a little jitter) and the first moment of the outage; `StreamEngine.tick()` (new, called by `DownloadController.tick()` with the single-file engine's) resumes a job whose pause is over while the network gate is online and ends one that waited `outageGiveUpMs` (30 minutes) without a byte as FAILED / STALLED (the parts stay, Retry continues). A byte arriving clears the ladder.
- `Engine.waitOutOutage` (single files): the `alive > 1` refusal is replaced by the same evidence `retireExtra` uses: only when ANOTHER connection moved bytes after this one began failing (by `refusedEvidenceMs`) and recently (`refusedFreshMs`) is this a lone failure; when every connection fails the whole download waits.
- HTTP statuses (401, 403, 404, 410, 5xx) keep their own handling; so does an expired link.
Proof (JVM, failing test first): `StreamEngineTest` outage in the middle waits then carries on with identical bytes; a long outage ends as STALLED and Retry finishes from what was kept; a fresh download from a dead host fails fast with NETWORK; a 404 stays an HTTP failure. `EngineTest` three connections that all fail wait (WAITING_NETWORK) and finish with identical bytes (the old test only covered `parallel = false`).
