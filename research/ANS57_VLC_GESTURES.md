# ANS57 (owner message 57, 2026-11-16 17:04Z): VLC-style gestures in Kestrel's own players

## What the owner asked (verbatim in INBOX.md and TASKS.md ANS57a-f)
(a) A double tap on the left or right side (10 s back / forward) must close the controls: the film stays visible with nothing on top, "I'm not selecting video controls".
(b) "a more accurate fast forward or rewind in larger and smaller increments by long pressing around the middle of the screen and dragging right to fast forward and left to rewind. The farther I drag, the more it fast forwards or rewinds. Model it from [how] vlc media player handles this."
(c) "if I drag up or down on the left side of the video, it increases or decreases video brightness, and if I drag up or down on the right side of the video, it increases or decreases volume. Model this off how vlc media player handles this."

## How VLC for Android does it (read from the real source, 2026-11-16: videolan/vlc-android master, application/vlc-android/src/org/videolan/vlc/gui/video/VideoTouchDelegate.kt, GPL: ideas and numbers only, no code copied)
- Touch states: none / volume / brightness / seek-by-swipe / ignore / fast-play. DOWN stores the start point and whether it lies inside the allowed bounds (a 24 dp safety margin at the screen edges); MOVE decides; UP commits a seek.
- Vertical or horizontal: `coef = |dy / dx|`; a move with coef > 2 (steep) is vertical, anything else is horizontal. A vertical move only begins after the finger has gone 5 % of the screen range (yRange) up or down; from then on every move changes the level.
- Zones for the vertical drags: the left 3/7 of the width = brightness, the right 3/7 (x > 4/7) = volume, the middle 1/7 is a dead zone (VLC lets the person swap or switch off either one in its settings).
- Horizontal swipe = seek, `xcm = dx / xdpi * 2.54`. Under 1 cm nothing happens. The jump in milliseconds: `sign(xcm) * (600000 * (xcm / 8)^4 + 3000) / coef` (the power is 4: 1 cm = about 3.1 s, 2 cm = about 4 s... 4 cm = about 40 s, 8 cm = about 603 s = 10 minutes). The finer the finger moves ALSO vertically away from where it started, the smaller the jump: `coef = max(1, 2 * (|dy_from_start| / xdpi + 0.5))` (1 inch up or down = divide by 3). The jump is clamped so that the film stays between 0 and its length. The film is NOT moved while the finger drags: the info text shows the jump and where it lands, `+0:25 (1:23:45)` (a ` x0.3` tail names the fine factor), and the seek happens when the finger lets go.
- Volume: `delta = -(dy / yRange * audioMax * 1.25)` applied step by step as the finger moves (dragging up raises it), clamped 0..max of the music stream (200 % only with its boost setting on). Brightness: `delta = -dy / yRange * 1.25` of the window brightness (0..1; the start is the window's own value, else 0.5 when automatic brightness is on, else the system value / 255). VLC shows the level as text with a vertical bar and hides the controls while it does.
- Double tap: the left quarter and the right quarter seek by the person's chosen step (VLC default 10 s) and show the running total of the burst with the new time, the overlay going 750 ms after the last tap; the middle half plays / pauses.
- Long press: after 250 ms VLC plays at fast speed until the finger lets go (a different thing from what the owner asked; he asked for the long press to ARM the precise seek).
- Every gesture is a bit in one settings mask (volume 1, brightness 2, double tap 4, play 8, swipe seek 16, screenshot 32, scale 64, fast play 128).

## What Kestrel does (design, 2026-11-16)
1. ANS57a: the second tap of a double tap on a side hides the controls at once (they were shown by the first tap, TapMachine toggles at once since ANS53c) and they stay hidden through the burst; the "10 s" flash is alone on the picture. Core: ControlsMachine.seekGesture() called by PlayerModel's tap host.
2. ANS57b (the owner's own rule): a LONG PRESS (350 ms still, anywhere across the middle 70 % of the width) ARMS the precise seek: a short tick of the phone, the hint "Slide left or right to seek"; the horizontal drag then gives VLC's jump (the same formula and 1 cm dead zone), nothing is moved until the finger lets go, the info text is VLC's `+0:25 (1:23:45) x0.3`, a drag back to where it began is a cancel. A press that is let go without moving is not a tap. (VLC itself needs no long press; the owner wants it so that nothing seeks by accident and so that the page still scrolls under a plain drag in the player inside the page.)
3. ANS57c: a plain vertical drag (steep, coef > 2, after 5 % of the range) on the left = brightness, on the right = volume, zones 3/7 as VLC (dead zone in the middle), with VLC's 1.25 factor and the 24 dp edge margin, shown as an icon, a vertical bar and the percentage. In the player inside the page a plain drag scrolls the PAGE, so there the same gestures need the long press first (hold, then drag vertically or horizontally).
4. Volume = the music stream (AudioManager, never the page's own element); brightness = the window of the activity that shows the film (restored when the film ends: nothing is changed for good).
5. One pure core engine (core/player/PlayerGestures: states, thresholds, the VLC curve) drives the three hosts: NativePlayerActivity (full screen), InlinePlayer (inside the page), BrowserActivity.dispatchTouchEvent for a page film in the page's own full screen. Settings: a switch "Video gestures" (default on) in Settings.

## Lab finding (2026-11-16, runs 43918159286, 43921764750 and 43925029376)
- Vertical drags made with `input swipe` reached the engine on the real Gecko build (the volume / brightness bars and `gesture-level` lines, up and down: volume 27 -> 91 %, brightness 40 -> 92 % in run 43925029376). Every path written with `dd` to the emulator's touch device produced NO event: run 43925029376 printed `dd: /dev/input/event1: Permission denied` (the lab's adb shell is not root), and Android 10's `input` has no `motionevent` (that command is Android 11), so the lab could not rest a finger first. The first diagnosis (the UI thread handled the first move before the 350 ms timer rang) was wrong: no long press ever reached the app. The event-time arming added for it is still right for a busy tablet.
- The debug build now plays a finger path itself: geckoapp web/DebugTouch (real MotionEvents handed to Activity.dispatchTouchEvent on the app's own clock; `am start ... --es kestrel_debug touch --es kestrel_arg "D150,500 W500 M195,500 W2000 U418,500"`; ignored in a release build). Phone.hold_drag in tools/emu/soak.py builds the script. It proves the app-level rules (arming, preview, commit, hold-first inside the page); the system's own input pipeline is proven by the `input swipe` drags.
