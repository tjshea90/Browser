## Measured and NOT a lever (so nobody re-measures it)

- Extension listeners per request (reqbench.js, 1087 real requests from ted.com + cnn.com + ign.com, 100 rounds, this desktop): onBeforeRequest 3.1 us, onBeforeSendHeaders 2.4 us, onHeadersReceived 5.1 us per call. About 2.7 ms per page load on the desktop, so roughly 30 ms on the tablet for a 360-request page, spread over the whole load, and that is without the filter engine (SP1 measured that one). Nothing to cut.
- Titles: document.title vs og:title vs JSON-LD VideoObject.name vs h1 on 14 real pages. There is NO field that is always the cleanest: TED's og:title / JSON-LD drop the speaker ("Do schools kill creativity?", the title keeps "Sir Ken Robinson:"), archive.org's og:title equals its decorated title, h1 is "Verify to continue" on a bot-checked Vimeo page and "Latest Videos" on a listing. The earlier decision (RVW1 N3: keep document.title and clean it) STANDS; the real gaps were the site tails above. An "anchor" rule (cut the title after the page's own heading) was designed and REJECTED: `Dune - Part Two (2024)` with h1 "Dune" and `Popeye for President : Paramount Pictures` with h1 "Popeye for President" have the same structure, so it cannot tell a subtitle from a publisher.
- doraby.com: "Ozark - Doraby" with h1 "Ozark" and no og tags: the episode logic already names it right.

## Looked at and left alone, with the reason

- PB9 (buffer bytes by bitrate): the load control is built BEFORE the player knows which rung it will play (ABR), and a fixed 16 MiB ceiling is already most of this app's 25 MB heap budget. A bigger ceiling or a per-film one needs tablet numbers for stalls at 6-8 Mbit/s over the tunnel; nothing to prove it here.
- PB10 (release a backgrounded paused full-screen player): the rebuild path would re-buffer (2-3 s) every time the screen turns off and on again for a paused film, which is a worse everyday experience than keeping it. Not without the tablet.
- Guardian page: `maybe` probes against ad / tracker endpoints (doubleclick gampad, permutive, id5). In this harness no filter list is loaded, so on the tablet the requests that reach the wire are the ones Shields did NOT block; the app's probe is a 4 KB range read, budgeted at 12 per frame (background.js MAYBE_*). Not a measurable cost; no change.
