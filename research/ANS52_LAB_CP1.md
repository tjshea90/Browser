## ANS52g, the page-side codec and frame-rate policy: every refusal proven, 14 lab-script findings (not app faults)
- With the tablet's chip limits played by the debug intent `vfmt 0,0,tablet`: VP9 1080p60 / 1440p30 / 4K30 and H.264 4K30 are answered no by `isTypeSupported`, `canPlayType` and `mediaCapabilities.decodingInfo` alike while the engine itself says yes (baseline case `engine`); VP9 up to 1080p30 and H.264 up to 1080p60 stay as the engine says. `vfmt 1,0,tablet` (H.264 only): every VP9 row no. `vfmt 0,1,tablet` (30 fps): every row with a stated rate above 30 no. `vfmt 0,0,none` (limits not known): the page script refuses nothing (H.264 4K stays yes). The policy was already there at the page's first questions (1.5 s). Diagnostics: `refused so far: questions 216 ..., refused 112: above the decoder's limit 48, not H.264 40, above 30 fps 24; last: avc 3840x2160@30`: the counts come back through content script, background page and the app.
- The 14 FAILED checks were all one thing in the lab script: it compared the page script's VP9 `isTypeSupported` answer with the case `engine` = mode 2, whose engine PREFERENCE `media.mediasource.vp9.enabled` is forced true; in mode 0 the preference is cleared and the emulator (no hardware VP9) then answers no by itself. On the tablet a hardware VP9 decoder makes Gecko offer VP9 whatever the preference says. Fix: the debug intent's `tablet` preset also forces that preference on (GeckoEngine.applyVideo, debug builds only) and the kept-rows check ignores the `isTypeSupported` column of VP9 rows where the preference decides (soak.py cp_scene).
