## Checked and fine
- Content scripts as a per-frame tax [bench proxy, headless Chromium, blank frame]: the 13 scripts cost 5.3 ms compile + 0.9 ms run (doc_start isolated 2.1 ms of which content.js 1.4, doc_start MAIN 2.2, doc_idle 1.1), in line with RVW1's 6.3 ms; the second `vfind.js` entry is guarded (`window.KestrelVF = window.KestrelVF || ...`), 0.2 ms. LAB5 #5 (video group only where a film can exist) is still open with unchanged advice; I found nothing to add or contradict. 28 % of the extension's JS bytes are comments (146 of 517 KB, crude count): about 3 ms of lexing per process, not worth a minifier.
- Every touch, pointer, wheel and scroll listener in the extension is passive (content.js:398,421,563,910, guard-main.js:149-150, seekbar.js:248-250, videobar.js:236-249, turn.js:107-112); no setInterval at load (videobar.js:420 runs at 4 Hz only while its bar shows, turn.js:52 only during a turn); the gate observer scans each added root once.
- Cosmetic CSS: 490 tag-led selectors go up front (18 KB of CSS), 270 of them `a[href*=]` / `a[href^=]`. Headless Chromium proxy on a page with 600 anchors, 600 divs, 600 images: +1.3 ms style (1.0 ms of it the anchor rules) [bench], so roughly 8-15 ms on the tablet [est]: not worth a redesign. The token scheme is sound: 11,285 of 14,252 index buckets hold one rule, the largest is 304 (`ima3`), nothing else above 55. Global exceptions (57) make `looseCss` skip its cache (CosmeticIndex.kt:62-64, `exc.isEmpty()` is never true), costing about 1 ms per top document on the cosmetic thread [read]: leave it unless a Diagnostics number points there.
- Per-request glue in background.js is 3-10 % of the listener (SP2 numbers); Maps are bounded; the app is not asked per request once the engine is loaded.
- Touch path: dispatchTouchEvent allocates nothing per move, KestrelGeckoView asks for the detail result on DOWN only, Neon draws pre-built strokes (alpha step at 10 Hz, owner decision); onDraw methods reuse their Paint and Path objects.
- FilterUpdater: 14 conditional GETs on a MIN_PRIORITY thread, compile peak 19 MB above an empty heap and 5 MB retained (ADBLOCK_ENGINE.md, JVM) fits the 128 MB class; install is atomic.
- Disk and wake-ups: browser.db is WAL with synchronous NORMAL, the launch-time purge is one indexed statement on the nice-13 lane, history suggestions run on Io.fast with a 70 ms debounce (a LIKE scan of at most 5000 rows, about 10-25 ms per query on the A53 [est], off the UI thread); SharedPreferences are written at onPause and on Settings changes only; Health watchdog is one 500 ms sleep per calm beat, LastRun 15 s, CpuMeter 5 s (`/proc` shows only the app's own uid), the extension's timers are lazy (500 ms count flush, 5 s stats) with no setInterval.
- Memory: BackPictures 4 x about 750 KB, thumbnails 240 px RGB_565 (20), LiveBudget steps 250-1500 MB, VideoFlow cleared on close (P7), engine image read with one allocation (P11), TabStateStore capped at 96 K characters and rewritten only for states that changed.
- Assets: default.kfs 6.8 MB raw / 2.8 MB deflated, en.klx 4.0 -> 1.7 MB, psl.txt 148 -> 44 KB, the extension folder 553 -> 160 KB deflated: the deflate default is right for a 5 Mbit update; omni.ja stored and the catch-all baseline profile are already measured (LAB5, LAB7).
- Engine start from the saved image: create() is 16.5 ms with JIT and 21 ms jitless on this machine (SP7 is only its checksum and warm-up); `siteOf` and the page-hash memo are bounded.

## Not verified
- All per-frame content-script numbers are Blink eval times. Whether Gecko compiles a content script once per process (PrecompiledScript / script cache) and what per-frame instantiation and delazification cost on the A53 is unknown; only a per-script `performance.now()` pair in a debug build can say (LAB5 "measure first").
- That the tablet runs engine.check at interpreter or baseline speed is an inference from one paste (alpha27, 723 us, cold JIT included, 1 ms clock clamp) and an assumed 5x A53 factor; SP2 (b) is the cheap proof. If it is wrong the gain of SP1 on the tablet is the JIT figure (-35 %), not -45 %.
- Token-less rule count of the daily-updated set (bundled snapshot measured: 455 of 22,484 pattern rules; the extra tracker lists were not in the image I could read).
- Cost of `performance.now()` in an extension page under privacy.reduceTimerPrecision with jitter (SP2 c), and real GPU read-back time on the Mali-G52 (SP3).
- DNS: `trustedRecursiveResolverMode(TRR_MODE_FIRST)` (GeckoEngine.kt create()) needs a TLS connection to mozilla.cloudflare-dns.com (plus a native bootstrap lookup) before the first answer of a session and falls back after a timeout; on a flaky carrier hotspot each timeout is a delay before the page can even connect. The Network lab A/B (A9) exists and the owner has sent no numbers; I did not change or rank it (it is a privacy choice).
- Idea, sign unknown: a cold start on this tablet is dominated by paging the 118 MB libxul.so in from eMMC (LAB5_PACKAGING, COLD_START). A background thread started in Application.onCreate that reads the extracted library sequentially (Chrome's LibraryPrefetcher does the equivalent) would turn 128 KB-readahead demand faults into one stream, at the price of up to 118 MB of reads on a 2 GB device. Needs an A/B with a dropped page cache on the tablet or a lab start (marks `runtime`, `paint`); do not ship unmeasured.
- Nothing here ran on a device; the WebView build (:app) was not reviewed beyond the shared code.
