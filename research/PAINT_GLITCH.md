# GL1: parts of a page are not drawn - "an invisible line, and under the line is blank" (owner, 2026-11-21)
Owner, verbatim: "there is a strange glitch where some elements of pages are not drawn. For example, there may be an invisible line, and under the line is blank. I will send screenshots of this". STATUS: no screenshot has arrived (and the owner's screenshot upload is itself failing, IMG1). Nothing is proven; this file lists what could draw a hard horizontal edge with blank below it, and what each would look like, so the first screenshot decides.

## Suspects (code reading only)
1. A memory flush of the engine's picture caches: `gecko-prefs.txt` notes that Android's "low memory" tells Gecko to flush ALL decoded images and WebRender's caches (research/SCROLL.md). The tablet logs `mem trim` level 20 every few minutes with 356-1167 MB available (diag 2026-11-21). A page that does not repaint after the flush shows holes where images or layers were. Sign: the hole is where images / blurred or transformed layers were, a scroll or a tap redraws it, and `mem trim` / `engine` lines precede it in Diagnostics.
2. The page's own lazy loading: `dom.lazy-loading.margin.*` and `image.mem.surfacecache.size_factor` are tuned; an `IntersectionObserver` page that never reports the visible part leaves it blank. Sign: the same page is complete in Kestrel WebView or after a reload.
3. Shields hiding too much (cosmetic rules): a hidden container leaves a clean blank area with a hard edge. Sign: Shields down for the site (per-site switch) removes the hole; `cosmetic` lines in Diagnostics name the rule counts.
4. Our own overlays: the PV1 curtain (`BrowserActivity.curtain`, full-size, only during a session swap, down at first paint or 3 s), the Neon glow, the mini panel. A full-size curtain stuck would blank the whole page, not a band, so unlikely; a half-height band points at the bottom bars' layout instead.
5. The compositor on this GPU (Mali-G52 / MT8168B, hardware WebRender + GPU process by default): a tile that is never painted shows as blank with straight edges. Sign: happens on many sites, after scrolling fast or zooming, and goes away on the next full repaint.

READING: parts of text runs / animated pieces lose horizontal bands while the rest of the same frame is right. That is the signature of the GPU renderer's partial updates (WebRender picture-cache tiles / dirty rectangles, or a glyph / blob upload that did not complete) on a CPU-starved page, not of anything the page's CSS or our hiding rules do. The common factor of all three is a page that repaints small parts of itself while its main thread is 90 % late (claude.ai lateness 91 %, pornhub 74-77 %). The GPU here is a Mali-G52 under a GPU process (Processes list shows :gpu).
What cannot be proven without the tablet: which of partial present, batched texture upload or the shader pre-compile (gfx.webrender.precache-shaders = true since alpha20-ish) is the trigger. So the next build adds a Settings switch "Safe drawing" (engine prefs that turn those paths off) for a one-minute A/B and a Diagnostics `paint` line; the default is decided by the owner's answer.

## PERF7c step 1 (alpha92): "Safe drawing" (Settings > Network lab, default ON, applies at the next start)
Writes four engine start-up prefs from `GeckoPrefs.drawLines` (all verified to exist in GeckoView 160.0 libxul, tools/check-gecko-prefs.sh: 60 names): `gfx.webrender.scissored-cache-clears.enabled=false`, `gfx.webrender.batched-texture-uploads=false`, `gfx.webrender.max-partial-present-rects=0`, `gfx.webrender.allow-partial-present-buffer-age=false`. Each turns off a shortcut that updates only PART of a texture / frame (the signature of the screenshots). Diagnostics "Engine prefs" now reads them back (plus `pbo-uploads` and `layer-compositor`, read only, to learn the engine's own defaults).
HOW TO TELL IF IT WORKED: the next upload with the switch ON must show the four values in the read-back; if bands still appear with it ON, the shortcuts were not the cause: next candidates are `gfx.webrender.pbo-uploads`, `gfx.webrender.layer-compositor`, `layers.gpu-process.enabled` (GPU process), then software WebRender (`gfx.webrender.software`, slow on 4 A53 cores).

## 2026-11-22 (PERF10a, owner after alpha94: "still chopping off paint, words half invisible"): what the newest uploads prove and the next step
- The alpha94 uploads (175002 automatic, 221024 Send now) carry `engine draw-prefs scissored-cache-clears.enabled=false batched-texture-uploads=false max-partial-present-rects=0 allow-partial-present-buffer-age=false pbo-uploads=true layer-compositor=false software=false layers.gpu-process.enabled=true`: Safe drawing REACHED the engine and the bands still happen, so those four shortcuts were not the (only) cause. Still untouched: PBO texture uploads (`pbo-uploads=true`), the GPU process (`:gpu` is running), the hardware renderer itself.
- Mozilla's own bug history (read 2026-11-22): the same family of symptom (white bars over text, half-drawn lines, missing glyphs, hidden until zoom or select) hit Adreno 3xx / 308 GPUs (bugs 1513185, 1843749, 1874848) and a Mali-G71 (1558374, scissored clears, already off here). The causes were GPU-driver paths in WebRender (a tiled-rendering extension, texture uploads); the only user-side switch that made them go away in every report is `gfx.webrender.software = true` (CPU drawing). There is NO known pref that names a Mali-G52 text-band fault.
- So alpha95 does two things (PERF10b): (1) adds `gfx.webrender.pbo-uploads = false` to Safe drawing (uploads go through plain texture calls instead of mapped upload buffers: a timing-dependent partial row upload is the classic reason for a band missing from some glyph rows while the rest of the frame is right, and this tablet's page is busy 90 percent of its load); (2) adds Settings > Network lab > "Software drawing" (default OFF, applies at the next start): the engine draws with the CPU, which no GPU driver can corrupt, at a cost in speed on the four slow cores. The owner A/Bs it: if bands vanish with it ON, the GPU path is the cause and a per-device decision can follow. `engine draw-prefs` already reads `software=` back.
- NOT run on the tablet. A band that survives BOTH is not the engine's drawing: next suspects are then our own overlay views (curtain / look picture / Neon) over the GeckoView.
