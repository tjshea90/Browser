# GL1: parts of a page are not drawn - "an invisible line, and under the line is blank" (owner, 2026-11-21)
Owner, verbatim: "there is a strange glitch where some elements of pages are not drawn. For example, there may be an invisible line, and under the line is blank. I will send screenshots of this". Nothing is proven; this file lists what could draw a hard horizontal edge with blank below it, and what each would look like, so the first screenshot decides.

## Suspects (code reading only)
1. A memory flush of the engine's picture caches: `gecko-prefs.txt` notes that Android's "low memory" tells Gecko to flush ALL decoded images and WebRender's caches (research/SCROLL.md). The tablet logs `mem trim` level 20 every few minutes with 356-1167 MB available (diag 2026-11-21). A page that does not repaint after the flush shows holes where images or layers were. Sign: the hole is where images / blurred or transformed layers were, a scroll or a tap redraws it, and `mem trim` / `engine` lines precede it in Diagnostics.
2. The page's own lazy loading: `dom.lazy-loading.margin.*` and `image.mem.surfacecache.size_factor` are tuned; an `IntersectionObserver` page that never reports the visible part leaves it blank. Sign: the same page is complete in Kestrel WebView or after a reload.
3. Shields hiding too much (cosmetic rules): a hidden container leaves a clean blank area with a hard edge. Sign: Shields down for the site (per-site switch) removes the hole; `cosmetic` lines in Diagnostics name the rule counts.
4. Our own overlays: the PV1 curtain (`BrowserActivity.curtain`, full-size, only during a session swap, down at first paint or 3 s), the Neon glow, the mini panel. A full-size curtain stuck would blank the whole page, not a band, so unlikely; a half-height band points at the bottom bars' layout instead.
5. The compositor on this GPU (Mali-G52 / MT8168B, hardware WebRender + GPU process by default): a tile that is never painted shows as blank with straight edges. Sign: happens on many sites, after scrolling fast or zooming, and goes away on the next full repaint.
