alpha61: your message 60 (on alpha60: The video player works well. YouTube works well. A couple small improvements). (1) NO LONG PRESS any more for the video gestures, in Kestrel's player and in the player inside the page: drag sideways on the film to go forward or back (the farther you drag, the bigger the jump; drag also up or down for a finer step; the film moves when you let go), drag up or down on the left side for the brightness and on the right side for the volume. Inside the page a vertical drag that starts in the middle strip of the film, or on the page around the film, still scrolls the page. If you would rather scroll the page by dragging on the film, Settings > Gestures inside the page need a long press brings the alpha60 way back. (2) THE HINT now says what you can do where your finger is: rest a finger on the left side and it says Slide up or down for brightness, on the right side Slide up or down for volume, in the middle Slide left or right to seek; it goes away the moment you move, so it can no longer say seek over a volume drag. (3) QUALITY INSIDE THE PAGE: the player inside the page has a Quality button (left of the full-screen button) with the same list as the full-screen player: Automatic and every picture the stream offers; a pick is remembered for the next films inside a page (apart from the full-screen pick). Back closes the list first. (4) PLAYBACK TUNED FOR THIS TABLET (the reasons, and what I looked at and left alone, are in research/ANS60_PLAYBACK_OPTIMISATION.md): ten seconds that were already played stay in memory, so the 10 s back button, a double tap on the left and a drag to the left land at once instead of fetching the same seconds again over the hotspot; the player starts from a guess of 4 Mbit/s that fits your 5 Mbit/s hotspot (instead of ExoPlayer's higher guess) and keeps what it measured for the next film; a connection that goes quiet is given up after 10 s instead of 20; the Wi-Fi chip is kept awake while a film plays; the first film after you open Kestrel no longer pays for Android's codec list (it is asked once while Kestrel is idle). I cannot measure speed here (the emulator decodes in software on a fast link), so Diagnostics now measure it: for every film it records how long until it could play, how often and how long it waited for the network, the link speed the player measured, the picture size it settled on and the pictures dropped; the Kestrel's own player line in Diagnostics has the totals. TESTED: all the unit tests and the 576 page-script tests pass, and the emulator lab ran the whole video program on the real Firefox engine (an Android 10 emulator, not your tablet): 367 checks, 0 failed, no crash. That includes the gestures in Kestrel's own player AND inside the page without any long press (27 of 27: a plain sideways drag seeks, the hint says brightness on the left, volume on the right and seek in the middle, the volume and brightness drags, a double tap = 10 s with the controls going away, the middle strip of the film still scrolls the page, and the old long-press way works behind the Settings switch), the Quality button and list inside the page (10 of 10: a real tap opens it, a pick changes the picture, it closes and the film plays on), Kestrel's own player after the quality code was moved (43 of 43), the film inside the page (21 of 21), press to play (40 of 40), web videos in Kestrel's player (53 of 53), the page video formats (101 of 101) and the picture size cap (28 of 28).
