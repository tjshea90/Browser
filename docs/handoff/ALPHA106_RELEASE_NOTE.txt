alpha106: Firefox 161, released today. Kestrel G now runs on the Firefox 161 engine (GeckoView 161.0.20261118180411, the newest, from 2026-11-24) instead of 160. It carries Mozilla's security fixes of this month (advisory 2026-131) and Mozilla's speed-up for pages that blur or frost things behind page elements. The site-fixes add-on is updated to 161.0 as well. FOR YOUR TABLET: every engine setting Kestrel writes was checked against the new engine: all of them still exist and nothing Kestrel uses was removed. The engine is Mozilla's own optimised 32-bit build for your processor. Firefox 161's new settings (new path drawing, a Rust font reader, do not decode pictures that are not on screen) are NOT switched on blindly: the next diagnostics read their default values on your tablet, and the Auto-tune that is being built next will test each one on the tablet itself. TESTED: it compiles against the new engine, the unit tests and browser-engine tests pass, and the emulator test (a pretend Android 10 tablet) started the new engine and loaded pages. NOT run on your tablet. The first start after the update takes longer, and your tabs reopen from their addresses (the old engine's saved page states are not reused with a new engine). ALSO INSIDE, SWITCHED OFF: the first parts of Auto-tune, the automatic settings tester that is being built next (it has no screen in Settings yet, so you will see no change).
