alpha101: the browser now keeps its warm memory when memory is not short. I measured your tablet and read how the engine, Chrome and Android handle memory (research/RAM_FOR_SPEED.md). (1) FINDING: Android sends the browser a critical low memory warning about four times a day, and one every time you leave the app, while 340 to 800 MB are still free (all 33 of the last warnings of the strongest kind came with at least 340 MB free). The page engine obeys every one of them: it throws away its decoded pictures, its frozen Back pages and, through a clean up run, the compiled script code of the pages you use, which is exactly what makes a heavy page fast when you come back to it. (2) CHANGE: those warnings now pass through a gate. A warning is held when 350 MB or more are available, the system itself does not say low memory, the engine was not ended in the last 6 hours, and fewer than 6 were already held in the last half hour. Anything else is passed on exactly as before, so real shortage is still answered. Settings, Keep the engine's memory when it is not short, turns it off. Diagnostics shows the line Memory signals to the engine with the held and passed on counts. (3) NOT DONE, with reasons: memory is not your main limit (median 481 MB free at page loads, 2 engine kills in 14 days; the page thread's processor is), a second page process would cost 65 to 95 MB and your numbers show no sign it would help (first paint with 2 live tabs 1.8 s, with 1 tab 2.0 s), keeping the app process alive after Android closes its screen would gain 1 to 2 seconds for 300 MB, a bigger Back cache was already flagged as too much. Not run on the tablet: the gate is tested in the unit tests and compiled into the engine build; the first upload shows whether warnings are held (mem trim-held lines), whether engine kills stay at about one a week, and the speed gain is a hypothesis until then.
