alpha73: the rest of the code tasks from the whole-browser review that came with alpha72, and from now on every finished version is released without asking. VIDEO FINDING: a player frame that a site removes and replaces now takes its download rows with it (the old server no longer stays listed); what the page says about a video that redirects (its length, its picture, that it played) now reaches the file; the variant and audio playlists of a stream are no longer counted as extra videos and no longer use up the early check; a slow check started by a server you already left can no longer bring its row back; the same file on two hosts is one row. DOWNLOADING: a server that answers in pieces without saying the total size no longer ends the download after the first piece; a big single part that dies in the middle (a one file stream) continues where it stopped instead of starting over; DASH manifests that put the part rules on the Period work, and when a stream cannot be listed the message says whether it is the layout or the format; an encrypted start section of a stream is decrypted before it is written; file names like Dune.Part.Two.2024 get .mp4 and download.php served as a video becomes download.mp4; on Android 15 and later the downloads pause cleanly when the system reaches its 6 hour background limit, with a notification saying why (instead of the app being killed). SECURITY: the login prompt now names the server that is really asking, and says so when it is not the page you are on. SPEED: the pop-up guard only looks over a page when it has a frame that could be a fake layer, the session save runs on the faster lane so it survives a kill better, and the watchdog wakes half as often while the app is calm. DECIDED NOT TO DO, with reasons in research/REVIEW_2026-11-20*.md: lazy loading of the scriptlet library (measured: parsing is 1.6 ms and cannot be avoided), the Watch Dogs title rule, and the secret scanner exemption. WHAT I COULD NOT PROVE ON THE TABLET: the video finder fixes are tested on pages modelled on real players, not on the real Vimeo, vidsrc or doraby; the login prompt, the redirect wiring and the Android 15 limit notification are compiled, linted and unit tested only (the Android 15 limit needs an Android 15 device); the speed changes are not measured on the tablet. Still open and not code: your decision on the engine own background traffic (Firefox settings and Safe Browsing lists), and the one shader setting that only your tablet can judge.
