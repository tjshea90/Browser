alpha69: your messages after alpha68. (1) THE DOWNLOAD MANAGER NO LONGER POPS UP BY ITSELF. I found two causes by reading the code. After you tapped a download notification once, Android kept that tap on Kestrel and played it again every time it restarted the app, so the Downloads list opened on its own; now only a real tap on a notification opens it, and a replay after a restart is ignored. And the small download window treated every old download as new when Kestrel started (and any download that carried on by itself); now it comes up only for a download you start or resume yourself, and a stale Download started message cannot appear at start. (2) THE DOWNLOAD WINDOW NOW FLOATS LIKE THE KEYBOARD: drag it by its title bar, or long press anywhere on it and carry it with your finger; drag the corner mark at its bottom left to resize it; double tap that corner to put it back; Menu, Settings, Reset the downloads window does the same. Place and size are remembered. (3) VIDEO SERVERS THAT STAYED ON LOADING (doraby: vidsrc, vidfast and the others): the press-to-play rule held the video of an embedded server until you pressed inside that embedded player, but your tap on the site own play or server button never counts inside it, so the player waited for ever without an error. Now a tap you make on the page opens the video of the servers it leads to, once, and only for a video box big enough to be the main player; a page that starts a film with no tap from you is still held. (4) VIDEO NAMES: the name now comes from clues closest to what you pressed: the address of the player (season and episode in it), the highlighted episode in the site list, then the page title, so Ozark season 1 episode 2 is named Ozark S01E02 and not just Ozark; you can still edit it in the Save video sheet. (5) VIDEO QUALITY IN FULL SCREEN: the Quality button now lists the choices of the site own player (YouTube and the common hls, dash, jw and video.js players) and your pick goes back to that player, so the film carries on with no gap. (6) THE YOUTUBE CRASH: I could not prove the cause. Your Diagnostics has no crash note and no engine crash report, so it was not a Java crash and not one the engine could report; the most likely cause is Android taking the whole app for memory on a 2 GB tablet while YouTube was loading, but that is a conclusion by elimination. So this release makes the next time provable: Diagnostics has a new line Last run that says whether the last run ended on purpose, in the background, or while on screen, and then the likely cause, the free memory and the last events. If it happens again, open Menu then Diagnostics as soon as the app is back and send me the text. NOT proven on the tablet: any of this on the real sites (doraby servers, YouTube quality, the real episode list of doraby), the feel of moving and resizing the window with a finger, the download list no longer opening after a restart, and the Last run line on real hardware; everything above was checked with tests and on a simulated Android tablet with the real Firefox engine, using test pages that behave like those sites. If a server still stays on loading, or the Quality list is empty on YouTube, send Diagnostics right after.
