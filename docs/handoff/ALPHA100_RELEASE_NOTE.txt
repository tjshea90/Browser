alpha100: a faster start after a long break, and the first real numbers for laggy animations and slow bot checks. (1) START: Firefox for Android opens on its start screen when you left it 4 hours ago or more, and loads the old tab only if you ask; Chrome does the same with its start page. Kestrel now does this too: if you open it 4 hours or more after you left it, it starts on Home with one line, Continue and the page title; tap it and the page loads (Back and typing a new address work as before). Your diagnostics show why it matters: a heavy web app in front cost 12 to 27 seconds of the tablet processor at every start, median 19 s to the first picture. Less than 4 hours away: the page loads at once as before. Settings, After a long break, wait with the last page, turns it off. Background tabs were already loaded only when opened, as in every major browser. (2) ANIMATIONS: nothing was ever measured on your tablet for page animations, so every page now reports how many frames it drew in the 2.5 seconds after it loaded and how many were late; the report I read splits this by Software drawing and Calmer redrawing, so the next upload shows what each costs. Two switches already exist if you want to try now: Settings, Network lab, Calmer redrawing (a steady 30 frames a second, also for scrolling) and Settings, Video, Calm pages (pages that honour reduce motion stop their animations). (3) BOT CHECKS: pages that are a Cloudflare style Just a moment check, or hold a check box, are now marked, and the time you spend on the full page check is reported, so the first real number for how slow they are arrives with your next upload. (4) RESEARCH: I read what Chrome, Firefox, Safari and the low memory modes do for fast loading, smooth video, smooth animations, and tab and Back caching; almost all of it Kestrel already has (preconnect, speculative loading, caches, back and forward cache, tab budget, hardware video decode); the table with sources is research/BROWSER_TECHNIQUES.md.
