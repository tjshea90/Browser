alpha66: your Swoop keyboard and Kestrel are linked. The small navigation panel now opens your Swoop keyboard floating over the page when you tap its bar, if Swoop is the keyboard you have chosen in Android: you type into the page's text box or the address bar the normal way, and Swoop learns what you type, so there is ONE memory for both. Settings has a switch, Use the Swoop keyboard from the panel (on): off, or Swoop not chosen, and the panel's own small keyboard opens as before. That small keyboard got the Swoop fixes: a quick swipe to the next key is now a glide and not one letter, one special character brings the letters back at once, and its word engine is the same code as Swoop's (the bigger word list, rare words such as tui no longer beat common ones). Everything else is alpha65 unchanged. NOT proven on the tablet: Kestrel opening Swoop and making it float (the phone and the tablet need Swoop 0.5.0-alpha2 or newer chosen as the keyboard); tell me exactly what you see if the keyboard does not appear or does not float.
