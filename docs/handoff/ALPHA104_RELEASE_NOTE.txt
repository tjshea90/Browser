alpha104: tab reloads, and the clip page. I read your two alpha103 uploads. (1) TABS RELOAD: the tab that kept going to sleep was your heavy chat page (it takes 13 to 23 seconds to paint again, 36 seconds to finish). Your other two live tabs were private tabs, which can never sleep, and the setting Tabs kept ready, Few (2), counted them, so the regular tab paid for them every time, even with 944 MB free. Now that number limits your regular tabs only, private tabs do not count against it, free memory still limits all live tabs (and now also caps a fixed number, so Many no longer ignores a memory shortage), and when a tab has to sleep the cheapest one to bring back goes first: a tab whose last page took 8 seconds or more to load is the last one put to sleep. (2) THE CLIP PAGE: the uploads show clips dropping 40 to 99 percent of their frames while three or four played within seconds of each other, and the app's own window stalling up to 0.98 seconds. Two clips at a time, the alpha102 limit, was still too many for this tablet, so now one clip plays at a time, and none while you scroll the page; they start again half a second after you stop. (3) TYPING AND SCROLLING: typing in text boxes waits for the page's own next picture (up to 1.2 seconds on busy pages, while the keyboard's own hand-over takes 16 to 24 milliseconds), and scrolling shows the same thing: pages draw about 6 pictures a second right after they load. The clip change removes the biggest source of late pictures on the clip page; the rest is the pages' own scripts on four weak cores, so there is no separate fix. One setting to try, no code: Settings, Appearance, Scroll feel: yours is Standard (Firefox's own), the least pre-drawing; the default is Smoother. Not run on the tablet: the tab rule and the clip limiter are tested in unit tests and in a desktop browser engine only. Said plainly: a heavy page that does have to reload still takes 13 to 23 seconds; the change makes it sleep far less often, not load faster.
