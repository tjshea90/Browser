alpha60: your messages 55, 57, 58 and 59 (the player inside the website; video gestures like VLC; staying logged in when you switch apps). (1) NEW, message 55: when you press play on a video in a web page, Kestrel's own player now plays INSIDE the page: it is drawn over the page's own video box, with the page above, below and around it. Scroll the page and the film moves with it; drag on the film and the page scrolls. Tap the film for its controls: play and pause, 10 seconds back and forward, the time bar, mute, a full-screen button (the same film goes on in the full-screen player at the same second; Back returns to the page) and an x that closes it. A long press that you hold on for about a second opens the film's download options. Back closes the film first. It stops by itself when you leave the page, switch tab, turn the tablet, open the tab list, Settings or Downloads, or scroll the film completely out of view (mostly out of view = paused). It still opens full screen, as in alpha59, where the film cannot be placed safely: a video inside a frame (an embedded player), a box that does not move with the page (a fixed or sticky header, a carousel or a scrolling box), a page you have pinch-zoomed or that is laid out for a desktop, a small thumbnail. Settings > Play web videos inside the page turns it off. (2) NEW, message 57, modelled on VLC for Android (I read VLC's own code): a double tap on the left or right side jumps 10 seconds and now puts the controls away, so the film stays clear. For a more accurate jump: rest a finger on the film for about a third of a second (you feel a tick), then drag right to go forward or left to go back: the farther you drag, the bigger the jump (about 3 seconds for 1 cm, about 40 seconds for 4 cm, 10 minutes for 8 cm), drag a little up or down at the same time for a finer step, and the film moves when you let go; VLC's text such as +0:25 (1:23:45) shows where it will land. Drag up or down on the left side for the brightness and on the right side for the volume (the strip in the middle does nothing, as in VLC), with a bar and a percentage; the brightness goes back to the tablet's own when the film ends. In the player inside the page a plain drag still scrolls the page, so there you hold first and then drag. They work in the full-screen player, in the player inside the page and in a page's own full screen with Kestrel's controls. Settings > Video gestures turns them off. (3) FIXED, messages 58 and 59 (switching to another app and coming back): when Android asked Kestrel for memory after you had left it, Kestrel closed the page in front, so on your way back it reloaded and a login that only lived in that session was gone (in a private tab the engine also forgets every private login the moment its last private page closes). Now the page in front is no longer closed by that, in a private tab or a regular one: a regular tab is only put to sleep when Android is about to end background apps AND you have been away 30 minutes or more, a private tab never is, and one hidden private page is kept open as long as any private tab exists so the private logins stay. Other tabs behind the one in front can still sleep to save memory, as before. If Android itself ends Kestrel while you are away (the tablet has only 2 GB) the pages come back from their saved history, but private tabs and logins that only lived in memory are gone: nothing can prevent that, and Diagnostics now say whether the screen was swiped away or taken by the system. When the system takes the screen but keeps the app, Kestrel no longer ends its own process 2 seconds later (it waits 2 minutes), so the way back is not a cold start. (4) For the slow Cloudflare check and the typing that lagged (message 58): Kestrel own page scripts (about 450 KB, for every page and frame) no longer run inside the human-check frames of Cloudflare, hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA, Arkose, DataDome and PerimeterX, so the check has the tablet one engine process more to itself. I could not measure this lag here: after a slow check please send the Diagnostics (it lists the CPU by process and the stalls). (5) The alpha59 fixes (the black Yahoo picture, a new video starting from its own beginning) are in this release too.
