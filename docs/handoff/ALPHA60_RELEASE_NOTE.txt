alpha60: your message 55 (the player inside the website). (1) NEW: when you press play on a video in a web page, Kestrel's own player now plays INSIDE the page: it is drawn over the page's own video box, with the page above, below and around it. Scroll the page and the film moves with it; drag on the film and the page scrolls. Tap the film for its controls: play and pause, 10 seconds back and forward, the time bar, mute, a full-screen button (the same film goes on in the full-screen player at the same second; Back returns to the page) and an x that closes it. A long press on the film opens its download options. Back closes the film first. It stops by itself when you leave the page, switch tab, turn the tablet, open the tab list, Settings or Downloads, or scroll the film completely out of view (mostly out of view = paused). (2) STILL FULL SCREEN, as in alpha59, where the film cannot be placed safely: a video inside a frame (an embedded player), a box that does not move with the page (a fixed or sticky header, a carousel or a scrolling box), a page you have pinch-zoomed or that is laid out for a desktop, a small thumbnail. Settings > Play web videos inside the page turns the new way off (always full screen). (3) The alpha59 fixes (the black Yahoo picture, a new video starting from its own beginning) are in this release too.
