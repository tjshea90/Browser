alpha60: your messages 55 and 57 (the player inside the website; video gestures like VLC). (1) NEW, message 55: when you press play on a video in a web page, Kestrel's own player now plays INSIDE the page: it is drawn over the page's own video box, with the page above, below and around it. Scroll the page and the film moves with it; drag on the film and the page scrolls. Tap the film for its controls: play and pause, 10 seconds back and forward, the time bar, mute, a full-screen button (the same film goes on in the full-screen player at the same second; Back returns to the page) and an x that closes it. A long press that you hold on for about a second opens the film's download options. Back closes the film first. It stops by itself when you leave the page, switch tab, turn the tablet, open the tab list, Settings or Downloads, or scroll the film completely out of view (mostly out of view = paused). It still opens full screen, as in alpha59, where the film cannot be placed safely: a video inside a frame (an embedded player), a box that does not move with the page (a fixed or sticky header, a carousel or a scrolling box), a page you have pinch-zoomed or that is laid out for a desktop, a small thumbnail. Settings > Play web videos inside the page turns it off. (2) NEW, message 57, modelled on VLC for Android (I read VLC's own code): a double tap on the left or right side jumps 10 seconds and now puts the controls away, so the film stays clear. For a more accurate jump: rest a finger on the film for about a third of a second (you feel a tick), then drag right to go forward or left to go back: the farther you drag, the bigger the jump (about 3 seconds for 1 cm, about 40 seconds for 4 cm, 10 minutes for 8 cm), drag a little up or down at the same time for a finer step, and the film moves when you let go; VLC's text such as +0:25 (1:23:45) shows where it will land. Drag up or down on the left side for the brightness and on the right side for the volume (the strip in the middle does nothing, as in VLC), with a bar and a percentage; the brightness goes back to the tablet's own when the film ends. In the player inside the page a plain drag still scrolls the page, so there you hold first and then drag. They work in the full-screen player, in the player inside the page and in a page's own full screen with Kestrel's controls. Settings > Video gestures turns them off. (3) The alpha59 fixes (the black Yahoo picture, a new video starting from its own beginning) are in this release too.
