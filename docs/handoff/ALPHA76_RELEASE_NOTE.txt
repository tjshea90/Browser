alpha76: cleaner download names and a cleaner video list, measured on real pages this time (ted.com, cnn.com, npr.org, vimeo.com, wikimedia, archive.org, abcnews.com). DOWNLOAD NAMES: the site's own name no longer stays in a saved file's name when the site has a short name or a two-word tail. A page called Sir Ken Robinson: Do schools kill creativity? | TED Talk used to be saved with TED Talk at the end, Latest Videos | CNN with CNN, Tiny Desk : NPR with NPR, and Vimeo Staff Picks on Vimeo with on Vimeo; Big Buck Bunny - Video Dailymotion, File:Big Buck Bunny 4K.webm - Wikimedia Commons and similar titles are cleaned the same way. A real title that only contains such a word (Ted Lasso) is left alone. VIDEO FINDING: an advertising video that Google serves from its own video servers (seen next to the real film on ted.com, a 15 second clip) is no longer offered as a file. A live stream that hands out its pieces as plain numbered mp4 files (abcnews.com live: one 1 MB file every few seconds, each listed as a video of its own) no longer fills the list with those pieces; three or more numbered files of one folder are treated as the parts of one stream, and a piece you long-press or a player really plays is still kept. Quality names (movie_1080.mp4), years and separate films are never taken for parts. PLAYBACK: dragging the brightness bar in Kestrel's own player now changes the screen only when the brightness really takes a new step (the screen has 256), instead of re-laying out the window at every touch event, so the level bar and controls stay smoother during the drag. MEASURED AND LEFT ALONE: the extension's per-request code costs about 3 to 5 microseconds per request on a desktop (roughly 30 ms per page on the tablet, spread over the load), so there was nothing to speed up there; the page title stays the source of the name (no single field was cleaner on real pages). NOT run on the tablet: everything here is checked by unit tests built from real page data and by headless-browser measurements; the title and parts rules have not been tried on a real site in Kestrel itself.
