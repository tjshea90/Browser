alpha86: private tabs checked end to end, and every hole found is closed. Most of it was already safe (history, saved tabs, page pictures, the downloads list, keyboard words, the Recents picture, warm-up connections), but I found seven places where something from a private tab could be saved or sent. (1) Changing Shields for a site from a private tab saved that site's name on the tablet; now the change lasts only while a private tab is open and is never written or backed up. (2) The Diagnostics Engine line printed the address of the front page even in a private tab, and the live diagnostics could have uploaded it to your private GitHub repository; now nothing is built or sent while a private tab is open or for two minutes after the last one closes. (3) Other diagnostic lines could name sites; now a safety net hides every site name and address in them while a private tab exists, and private tabs are not even counted. (4) The engine's crash reports kept a memory copy and the page address for up to 7 days; they are now deleted or cut at start and after every engine crash. (5) The keyboard learned where your thumb lands even in a private tab; not any more. (6) The find-in-page box and the video rename box now tell the keyboard not to learn. (7) Firefox's Safe Browsing asks Google about a short code of an address, in private tabs too; it is now paused while a private tab exists, which also means regular tabs have no malware or phishing address check during that time. What cannot be closed: the website you visit and its helpers see your visit, DNS lookups, your phone hotspot carries all traffic, and the Firefox engine's own private storage is relied on rather than tested here. Diagnostics now has a Private tabs line that shows the safety net working. NOT run on the tablet.
