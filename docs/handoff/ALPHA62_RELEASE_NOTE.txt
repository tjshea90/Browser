alpha62: your message 61 (a video page played an advertisement and you could not get to the real video). Cause found by reading the code: an advertisement before a film plays on the same video box as the film, so when you pressed play Kestrel's own player was given the ADVERTISEMENT; the page's own ad code never saw its ad finish, so it never went on to the film, and pressing play again gave Kestrel's player the same advertisement again. Fixed three ways: (1) when the page's player says an ad is playing, Kestrel's player does not take it, the page plays its own ad and then the film; if the film is already known it goes to Kestrel's player straight away. (2) A video address that names an advertisement (ads, advert, preroll, vast and similar words) is left to the page too. (3) Safety net for ads nobody could recognise: a short clip (up to 2 minutes) that Kestrel's player played to its end is not handed over a second time, so press play again and the page plays it and moves on to the real video. Cost: an ad Kestrel could not recognise plays once in Kestrel's player and a second time in the page. NOT tested on your tablet or on a real site: the Firefox engine in an Android 10 emulator played two test pages with an advertisement before the film on the same video box (one that says it is an ad: not taken over, the page plays the ad then the film; one that does not: taken once, then the second press goes to the page and the film plays), plus the automatic test suites. Not run on a real site or a real ad. If it still happens, tell me the site name.
