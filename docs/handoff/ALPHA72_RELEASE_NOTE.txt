alpha72: your message to review the whole browser and make it faster, lighter, safer, working on more sites, with a video downloader that finds and names videos better. Four reviews read the code (video finder, downloader and names, security, speed): about 70 findings, most fixed with tests, listed in research/REVIEW_2026-11-20*.md. (1) FINDING VIDEOS: players inside frames (the doraby servers) were losing the facts that tell a film from an ad; a stream named in the page and then fetched by the player never counted; DASH and disguised playlists never got the early ad / live / protected check; Facebook style ranged files were offered as one slice. New: video addresses inside JSON answers (Vimeo, JW Player, Wistia style), videos inside shadow DOM, addresses in data attributes, whole addresses whose name has mp4 and then m3u8, quality labels of source lists, short clips that really played. YouTube fragments and numbered segment files are no longer listed. (2) DOWNLOADING: reading a playlist or a key now retries like a segment instead of failing the whole download; servers that ignore ranges no longer cut segments short; one stuck part no longer lets the rest pile up on disk; a playlist that redirects resolves against the real address; the last second of some DASH streams and the audio language are fixed. (3) NAMES: Artist - Song keeps both parts; quality tags, ids and (3) tab counters are no longer used as names; emoji are cut safely. (4) SECURITY: the notification Open button no longer skips the installer check; a web page can no longer make the browser probe devices on your home network; private tab addresses are no longer written to crash notes; links can not drive Kestrel own screens; backslash address tricks are fixed; the secret scanner is stricter. (5) SPEED: pages without video no longer scan after every tap, closed tabs free their memory, the page rescan on return only runs when something changed, the filter list loads with half the peak memory, the ad filter skips impossible positions (measured only about 2 to 9 percent on a desktop). ALSO IN THIS BUILD, waiting for this go: the Diagnostics first start after an install line and the picture of a page that never finishes loading. WHAT I COULD NOT PROVE ON THE TABLET: the video finder fixes are tested on pages modelled on the real players, not on the real Vimeo, vidsrc or doraby; the Android side edits are compiled and unit tested only; the speed gains are not measured on the tablet.
