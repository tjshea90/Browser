alpha116: a small follow-up to alpha115 (alpha115, with the Proton Drive download fix, is inside this build, so install this one even if you have not installed alpha115). WHAT CHANGED: (1) A download that fails now leaves a trace in the diagnostics: a counter for each reason and one line with the reason, the answer of the server, the site and how far it got. Your failed Proton Drive download left nothing at all, so the diagnostics could not even show that downloads were failing. Downloads from private tabs leave nothing, as always. (2) A privacy slip of alpha115 is fixed: its new download counters also counted downloads from private tabs (only a number, never a site, but your rule is that a private tab leaves nothing behind). They now skip private downloads. (3) The Downloads screen no longer says In progress over a failed download: failed downloads and ones that wait for a decision have their own heading, Stopped, above the Finished ones. WHAT THE NEWEST DIAGNOSTICS SHOWED (build alpha114, 60 uploads): nothing new beyond the known slow spots: the first page after a cold start takes 16 to 19 seconds, claude.ai is limited by its own thousand small scripts, 15 engine kills (12 while the app was away), and the first start after an update ended by itself in 27 of 27 updates (alpha114 and alpha115 changed how Kestrel steps aside during an update; the next update shows whether that worked). The notes are in research/ND6_DIAGNOSTICS_2026-11-28.md. NOT RUN: nothing of this was run on the tablet; the proof is the tests of the download engine and the build checks.
