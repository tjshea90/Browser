alpha80: the Back button. You said Back sometimes takes long while it sits on a picture of the page, that it glitched once, and that caching pages for instant reloading may be the answer. It was: the slow Backs were pages that tell the browser not to keep them (feeds, chat sites such as Gemini, dashboards, many news pages): the engine cannot keep those, so it loaded them again over the hotspot while Kestrel showed a saved picture. INSTANT BACK (Settings, Instant Back, on by default): such a page is now kept anyway, so Back shows it at once. Every normal visit still asks the site for the newest page; only Back and Forward use the kept copy. It is never used in a private tab, and never for mail, banking, payment, sign-in, account or health pages (and a list of well-known bank, payment, health-insurance and mail names). The kept copy lives in the browser cache on the tablet; Clear browsing data removes it. This was checked on the real Firefox engine in an emulator: the same page that used to be loaded again from the network on Back was kept and shown instantly, and the same page under a sign-in address was still loaded again, as intended. The picture is also better: it now shows a thin line along the top edge after one second and a note, Still loading and the site name, after four seconds, so it no longer looks frozen, and Kestrel keeps eight pictures instead of four so older pages have one too. THE GLITCH: one cause I could find and fix: the app copy of the page history can be one page behind right after a page opens, which could put the picture of the wrong page over the page you were going back to; the picture is now only used when that copy matches the page on screen. I cannot say that was your glitch. STILL SLOW: a page that was still loading when you left it, a page with a video player, and a page whose tab was freed to save memory cannot be kept and still load again. Diagnostics, line Back, now also says how many pages were made cacheable and how often Back was instant. NOT run on the tablet.
