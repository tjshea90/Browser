alpha70: your messages after alpha69 (the doraby crash, and the video rows that disappear). (1) THE CRASH ON DORABY: you told me the app just vanished to the home screen, so it was not a freeze. My own code only ends the app when it has nothing left to do, and it records that, so Android or the engine ended it from outside. I cannot say which yet: your Diagnostics showed no crash note, no engine crash report and about 1 GB of memory free, but the free-memory number does not count what video decoders and the five engine processes hold on a 2 GB tablet. So this release records the real evidence: every 15 seconds while the app is in front, and at every notable event, the note on disk now holds the memory of each part of the app (main, page, graphics, media), the system free memory and swap, and the kernel memory pressure when the tablet has it. Diagnostics line Last run then says, for the run that ended, how much memory the app held at its last moment and whether memory was low (Android memory killer) or fine (a crash that left no report). It also keeps the last three runs that ended badly instead of one, keeps the freeze lines if the screen ever hangs, and writes its note from its own thread so a hang cannot silence it. If the app vanishes again, open Menu then Diagnostics as soon as it is back and send me the text. (2) VIDEO ROWS NO LONGER DISAPPEAR: once Kestrel has read a stream and it runs 2 minutes or more (or a file is 40 MB or more, or you opened it), it is kept in the list when the player frame reloads or goes away, and when the same page loads again; it moves to the end of the list and is replaced only when another player offers a film of its own, or when you go to another page or another episode. Short clips, previews and advertisements are still hidden as before. (3) THE FINDER ASKS MORE POLITELY: when a video server answered 404 to the first way of asking (some servers do that instead of 403 when they dislike the request), Kestrel now tries up to two other ways, and it no longer asks again for page requests that were not plain reads. (4) TAB PREVIEWS ARE FOR THE SITE IN THE TAB: the preview of a tab was filed under the tab number only, never cleared when the tab went to another site, and a late picture could be stamped on the new site; now a preview is stored together with the page it shows, a tab that goes to another page loses its old preview at once, a picture that arrives after the tab moved on is thrown away, and a preview with no record of its page (all the old ones) is not shown; Diagnostics has a new line Tab previews that says how many are for the wrong page (should be 0). (5) COPY BUTTON ON THE ADDRESS BAR: press the address bar and a Copy button appears at its right end; one tap puts the whole address (or what you typed) on the clipboard and says Copied; the keyboard stays up. (6) TABS IN THE FLOATING NAVIGATION PANEL: the panel has a new Tabs button with the number of open tabs; it opens a list between the buttons and the address bar with a preview, title and site for every tab, the tab you are on outlined; tap a tab to switch to it, tap the x on its right to close it (the list stays open for the next one), tap New tab at the bottom to open a tab and the panel keyboard at once; more than five tabs scroll by dragging the list; the panel is a little wider (four buttons now). NOT proven on the tablet: how the wider panel and the tab list feel under your thumb; that the rows stay on the real doraby servers; vidsrc and 111movies themselves are NOT solved: I do not yet know why their players are not found. Please open one of them, wait until it plays, and send Diagnostics right then: the media lines (maybe, maybe-refused, maybe-hit, frame-gone, page-clear) show what Kestrel saw. Checked with tests and on a simulated Android tablet with the real Firefox engine, using test pages that behave like those sites.
